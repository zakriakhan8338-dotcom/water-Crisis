The Challenge in Khyber

Water Shortages Should Never Come as a Surprise.

Mountain communities around Landi Kotal rely on seasonal rainfall, springs, and shared tanks. Without early intelligence, small supply drops escalate into acute emergencies.

1. Delayed Shortage Reporting

By the time dry tanks or broken pipelines reach district authorities, families have already endured days of severe water scarcity.

2. Limited Local Monitoring

Remote villages like Loe Shalman and Michni Kandao lack continuous telemetry, leaving reservoir depletion undetected until critical thresholds.

3. Fragmented Community Info

Residents lack an accessible, multilingual channel in Pashto, Urdu, and English to share observations and receive actionable conservation guidance.

System Architecture

How AAB AI Works

A closed-loop intelligence pipeline combining human voice reports with environmental forecasting.

01

Collect

Gather community voice and text water reports in Pashto, Urdu, and English alongside local storage observations.

02

Analyze

AI correlates verified community reports with rainfall deficits, temperature spikes, and historical depletion rates.

03

Predict

Generate transparent 14-day village shortage risk scores (0–100) before tanks run completely dry.

04

Alert

Notify households, farmers, and local water committees with clear early warnings and water-saving actions.
